Official Server Rules

🛡️ Please read before participating
📜 Welcome to Jem.
Please read all of the following rules carefully. By participating in this community, you are expected to follow these guidelines and treat everyone with respect.
RULE #1

🤝 Be Respectful

Be respectful to everyone. Treat all members with kindness and basic respect.

RULE #2

🚫 No Politics

No political talks or political topics should be raised in the server.

RULE #3

🛑 Zero Tolerance Content

No racism, homophobia, or pedophilia. Usernames and statuses containing such content will result in an instant ban. Jokes made about these subjects will also result in an instant ban.

RULE #4

👤 Appropriate Usernames

No inappropriate usernames. Staff may change inappropriate usernames when necessary.

RULE #5

📛 No Spamming

Do not spam in the Discord server under any circumstance. Do not argue with moderators about whether something is or is not considered spam.

RULE #6

🎥 Respect Content Creators

Do not talk disrespectfully about other content creators.

RULE #7

🌐 English or Hindi Only

Please keep the server to English or Hindi. This requirement exists to help our moderation team. Failure to communicate in English or Hindi may result in a kick.

RULE #8

🔞 Age Requirement

If someone is found to be under 13 years old, that member will be banned according to Discord's Terms of Service.

RULE #9

🌟 Keep It Friendly

Keep language and topics friendly. We want everyone to feel safe and comfortable in this community.

RULE #10

🛡️ Respect Moderators

Listen to moderators. Arguing with or harassing moderators may lead to a mute, kick, or ban.

RULE #11

⚠️ Staff Discretion

If any of the above rules are violated, staff reserves the right to take the necessary action against the offender.

RULE #12

📖 Follow Discord Rules

All members must follow Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

RULE #13

⚙️ Moderation Guidelines

Please read the moderation guidelines below to understand how staff may handle rule violations.

⚙️ Moderation Guidelines

Our moderation team may take action when server rules are violated. The action taken depends on the situation and severity of the violation.

  • Warnings may be issued when appropriate.
  • Members may be muted for rule violations.
  • Members may be kicked when necessary.
  • Serious violations may result in a ban.
  • Staff may take necessary action to maintain a safe community.
💙 Use common sense.

Everything doesn't need to be mentioned in the rules. You are expected to use common sense and be humble and respectful towards each other, as well as welcoming towards new members.
